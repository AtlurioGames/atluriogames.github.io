Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your player account and all online data at any time. You do not need to explain why.

1How to request deletion

  1. Open the Game and go to Settings. At the very bottom you will see Player ID. Tap it to copy.
  2. Email atluriogames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” and paste your Player ID.
  3. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you can no longer open the Game, write to us anyway and describe how you played (device, approximate dates, Arena player name). We will do our best to find your account.

When the Game gets a Delete account and data option in Settings, you will also be able to delete everything right from the Game.

2What is deleted

  • your player account;
  • your cloud save (the online copy of your progress and settings);
  • your Arena data: player name, avatar, league, rating, results, answer records and leaderboard entries.

3What is not deleted

  • Progress stored on your device. To remove it, uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Results that are already part of a finished weekly league. They stay in that league’s history without your name or any link to you.
  • Data held by advertising partners under their own policies. To stop personalized ads, delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device (resetting it only replaces it with a new one). See the Privacy Policy for links to their policies.
