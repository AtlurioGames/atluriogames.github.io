Delete your account and data

You can delete your account right in the Game or ask us by email. You do not need to explain why.

1In the Game

  1. Open Settings and go down to Help.
  2. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

The Game deletes your cloud save, Arena data and player account right away, erases the progress on this phone and starts over. This needs an internet connection and cannot be undone.

2By email

If you cannot delete your account in the Game, write to us.

  1. Find your Player ID at the very bottom of Settings. It is 28 characters long. Tap it to copy.
  2. Email atluriogames@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”. Include your Player ID, your name in the Game and your approximate level.
  3. We check these details against your account, delete your data and reply to you. This takes up to 30 days, usually less.

A Player ID alone is not enough, because it is not a password. Your name and level show us that the request comes from the owner of the account. If they do not match, we will ask you to clarify and will not delete anything.

The easiest way is to write from the Game: Contact us in Help opens an email that already contains your Player ID.

3If the Game is no longer installed

If you still have your Player ID (for example, it is in any email you sent us from the Game), write to us as described in section 2. You do not need to install the Game again.

If you do not have it, install the Game again and sign in with the same Google Play Games account as before. Your account and Player ID come back, and you can delete everything in the Game.

Without a Player ID we cannot tell which account is yours: we do not keep players’ email addresses or real names. If you played without Google Play Games, reinstalling creates a new account, and the old one can only be found by its Player ID.

4What is deleted

  • your cloud save: the online copy of your progress, profile and settings;
  • your Arena data: profile (name, appearance, level, rating), duel history and the recordings of your runs that other players duel against, tournaments, league places and leaderboard entries;
  • your player account and its link to Google Play Games;
  • when you delete in the Game, also the progress on this phone.

5What is not deleted

  • Progress on your phone, if you asked us by email. If you open the Game again, it creates a new account and backs this progress up under it. To erase the progress, uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Your name and appearance in other players’ duel history. They stay there for a while: each player keeps their last 20 duels, and new duels replace old ones.
  • Places in league groups of past weeks. These groups are used to settle weekly results, are not shown to other players and are deleted automatically within 4 weeks.
  • Server logs. They record your Player ID without your name. Unity keeps them for 7 days and then deletes them.
  • Your Google Play Games profile. It is managed by Google: you can delete it in the Play Games app or in your Google Account.
  • Data held by advertising partners. They keep it under their own policies. To stop personalized ads, delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device (resetting it only replaces it with a new one). See the Privacy Policy for links to their policies.
