Privacy Policy

Atlurio: World Geography Quiz · Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Atlurio: World Geography Quiz (the “Game”) uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Atlurio Games, an independent developer based in Kazakhstan (“we”, “us”). If you have any question, write to atluriogames@gmail.com.

In short. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not sell your personal information. Your game progress is stored on your device and, for online features, on Unity Gaming Services. The Game shows ads from advertising partners, which use your device’s advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. You can delete your account and online data at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game keeps your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, supplies, achievements, appearance) and settings (language, sound, theme, notifications) on your device. This data stays on the device unless you use the online features described below.

The Game schedules local notifications (for example, when your supplies are full or a daily challenge is waiting). They are created on your device and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in the Game settings or in your Android settings.

2. Online features (Unity Gaming Services)

Online features run on Unity Gaming Services (“UGS”), provided by Unity Technologies. When you use them, the following is processed:

  • Player account. The Game creates an anonymous player account with a random player ID. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID is linked to this account so you can restore progress on another device. We do not receive your Google account email or password.
  • Cloud save. A copy of your game progress and settings is stored with your player account so it can be restored and kept in sync between devices.
  • Arena. If you play the Arena, we process your player name (which you choose), avatar and frame, league, rating, match results and the record of your answers (which question, which answer, and timing). Answer records are used to calculate results and to detect cheating. Other players can see your player name, avatar, league and results in duels, tournaments and leaderboards.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, UGS receives your IP address, device and app information and timestamps when the Game connects. This is used to operate the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

Unity’s handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads: optional rewarded videos (for example, to get supplies or double your coins) and, from time to time, full-screen ads before a results screen. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation by these partners:

  • ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Game Player and App User Privacy Policy;
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

To show, limit, measure and protect ads, these partners may collect your device’s advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and how you interact with ads. If you allow it, they may also use this data to show personalized ads.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads can be personalized, and you can change your choice at any time in the Game settings (“Ad privacy”). On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads; after deleting it, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads.

4. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we will delete the related data.

5. Why we use this information

  • to provide the Game and its online features (performance of our agreement with you);
  • to keep the Arena fair, prevent cheating and abuse, and keep the service secure (our legitimate interests);
  • to show ads that keep the Game free (our legitimate interests) and, where required by law, personalized ads only with your consent.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

6. How long we keep it

Online data (player account, cloud save and Arena data) is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, this data is deleted within 30 days, except that results already published in a finished weekly league may remain in that league’s history without your name or any link to you. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. To delete your account and online data, follow the instructions on Delete your account and data. For any other request, write to atluriogames@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. International transfers and security

Unity and our advertising partners may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, using the safeguards described in their policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to the Game’s online data is limited to the services that need it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the Game.
