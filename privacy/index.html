In short: we do not ask for your real name, email address or phone number. Your progress is stored on your device and automatically backed up to Unity Gaming Services under a player account. If you use Google Play Games, the Game links that account to it, so that your progress can be restored on another device. In the Arena, other players see the player name and appearance you choose. The Game shows ads from advertising partners, which use your device’s advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. You can ask us to delete your account and online data at any time.

This policy explains what information the game Atlurio: World Geography Quiz (the “Game”) uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game is developed by Atlurio Games, an independent developer based in Kazakhstan (“we”, “us”). If you have any question, write to atluriogames@gmail.com.

1Information on your device

The Game keeps your progress (levels, stars, cups, coins, hints, supplies, achievements, statistics, appearance) and settings (language, theme, sound, vibration, notifications) on your device.

The Game schedules local notifications (for example, when your supplies are full or a daily challenge is waiting). They are created on your device and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in the Game settings or in your Android settings.

2Player account and cloud save

Online features run on Unity Gaming Services (“UGS”), provided by Unity Technologies. When the device is online, the Game does the following automatically:

  • Player account. The Game creates an anonymous player account with a random player ID. We do not receive your Google account, email or password.
  • Google Play Games. On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game links your player account to it, so that your progress and Arena profile return when you install the Game on another device. For this the Game receives from Google your Play Games player ID and display name; we do not receive your email address or password. Your Google Play Games account is managed by Google, and you can change it in the Google Play Games settings. Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud save. The Game regularly backs up your progress, profile (appearance, player name and the Player ID shown in Settings), statistics and some settings (language, theme and whether extra content is shown) to your player account, so that they can be restored. Sound, vibration and notification settings stay on the device.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, UGS receives your IP address, device and app information and timestamps when the Game connects. Unity may also receive crash diagnostics from the game engine. This is used to operate the service, fix errors, keep it secure and prevent abuse.

3Arena

If you play the Arena, we also process your player name (which you choose), avatar, frame, banner and flag, level, league, rating, match results and the record of your answers (which question, which answer and its timing). Answer records are used to calculate results and to detect cheating. Other players can see your player name, appearance, level, league and results in duels, tournaments and leaderboards.

Unity’s handling of the data in sections 2 and 3 is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

4Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads: optional rewarded videos (for example, to get supplies or double your coins) and, from time to time, full-screen ads before a results screen. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay mediation by these partners:

  • ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Game Player and App User Privacy Policy;
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

To show, limit, measure and protect ads, these partners may collect: device identifiers (the advertising ID and the App Set ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, Android version, language, app version), interactions with the Game and with ads (such as app launches, taps and ad views), and diagnostics (crash logs and performance data). If you allow it, they may also use this data to show personalized ads.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads can be personalized. Where this applies, the Game settings also show “Ad privacy”, where you can change your choice at any time. On any Android device you can delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads; after that, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads (resetting it only replaces it with a new one).

5Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game’s online features, contact us and we will delete the related data.

6Why we use this information

  • to provide the Game, keep your progress safe and run its online features (performance of our agreement with you);
  • to keep the Arena fair, prevent cheating and abuse, fix errors and keep the service secure (our legitimate interests);
  • to show ads that keep the Game free (our legitimate interests) and, where the law requires it, personalized ads only with your consent.

We do not sell your personal information for money and we do not use it for purposes not described here. Under some US state privacy laws, allowing advertising partners to use device identifiers for personalized ads may count as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out of this at any time by deleting your advertising ID as described in section 4.

7How long we keep it

Online data (player account, cloud save and Arena data) is kept while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days, except that results already part of a finished weekly league may remain in that league’s history without your name or any link to you. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

8Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. To delete your account and online data, follow the instructions on Delete your account and data. For any other request, write to atluriogames@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9Where data is processed and how it is protected

Unity and our advertising partners may store and process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, under the safeguards described in their policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access to the Game’s online data is limited to the services that need it.

10Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the Game.
